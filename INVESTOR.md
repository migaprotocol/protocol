# MIGA Protocol

### A Sovereign Wealth Engine for the Persian Diaspora

## The Opportunity

**88 million Iranians. 8 million in diaspora. Zero financial infrastructure that represents them.**

The Persian community is one of the wealthiest, most educated diaspora populations globally—yet lacks any unified vehicle for collective capital formation, governance, or humanitarian coordination. Current remittance flows exceed **$1.3B annually** through fragmented, inefficient channels.

MIGA creates the first **decentralized sovereign wealth fund** for a stateless nation.

## Why This Structure

1. **No Dump Risk** — No team tokens, no VC unlocks, no advisor vesting. Zero sell pressure from insiders.

2. **Regulatory Clarity** — Structured as an NGO. Contributions fund humanitarian and governance initiatives, not speculative returns.

3. **Aligned Incentives** — Governance requires locking both MIGA (collateral) and PARS (participation). Whales without commitment cannot dominate.

4. **Quantum-Safe from Day One** — Post-quantum cryptography via Lux FHE. Contributors in Iran, Afghanistan, and other high-risk regions participate without exposure.

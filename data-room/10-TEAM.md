# Team

## MIGA Protocol: Core Contributors and Advisors

---

## Philosophy

MIGA is designed as a **community-owned protocol** with no traditional company structure. Core contributors are community members who have dedicated significant time to building the protocol.

**Key Principles:**
- No team tokens (0% allocation)
- Contributors participate same as everyone else
- Decentralized contributor network
- Governance will determine future compensation

## How to Contribute

### Open Contributor Roles

| Area | Needs | How to Apply |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Development | Rust, Solidity, TypeScript | GitHub PRs |
| Design | UI/UX, graphics | Discord #design |
| Content | Writing, video | Discord #content |
| Translation | Farsi, Turkish, Arabic | Discord #translation |
| Community | Moderation, support | Discord #community |

### Contributor Grants

Post-launch, DAOs will fund contributor grants:

**Grant Sizes:**
- Small (<$5K): Community content, small features
- Medium ($5K-$25K): Major features, audits
- Large ($25K-$100K): Core development, infrastructure

**Application Process:**
1. Submit proposal to relevant DAO
2. Community feedback period
3. DAO council review
4. Community vote
5. Milestone-based disbursement

## Transparency Commitment

### Public Information

- All contributor work is public (GitHub, forum)
- No private contributor channels for decisions
- Compensation will be disclosed post-launch
- Foundation financials audited annually

### Pseudonymity Note

Some contributors operate pseudonymously for:
- Personal security (Iran-related concerns)
- Professional considerations
- Privacy preferences

Pseudonymity does not impact accountability:
- Work product is public
- Contributions are tracked
- Reputation is established
- Legal accountability via Foundation

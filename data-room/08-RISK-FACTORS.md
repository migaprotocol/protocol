## Market Risks

### Price Volatility

**Risk:** The MIGA token may experience extreme price volatility.

| Factor | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|------------|------------|
| Crypto market cycles | High | High | Long-term holding thesis |
| Low liquidity periods | High | Medium | Liquidity incentives |
| Speculative trading | High | High | Governance focus over speculation |
| External market shocks | High | Medium | Treasury diversification |

**Details:**
- Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile
- MIGA may lose 90%+ of value during market downturns
- Past performance of other tokens does not indicate future results
- Token may never reach pre-sale pricing levels after launch

### Liquidity Risk

**Risk:** You may be unable to sell MIGA tokens at desired prices.

| Scenario | Probability | Impact |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Low trading volume | Medium | Unable to exit large positions |
| DEX pool imbalance | Low | High slippage |
| Market panic | Medium | Extreme price impact |
| Exchange delistings | Low | Reduced access |

### No Guarantee of Value

**Risk:** MIGA tokens may have no value.

- Tokens are not redeemable for any asset
- No guaranteed buyback or floor price
- Value depends entirely on market demand
- Protocol success does not guarantee token appreciation

## Technical Risks

### Smart Contract Vulnerabilities

**Risk:** Smart contracts may contain bugs or vulnerabilities.

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| Logic errors | Critical | Multiple audits |
| Reentrancy attacks | Critical | Standard patterns |
| Oracle manipulation | High | Chainlink/Pyth integration |
| Upgrade vulnerabilities | High | Immutable core, timelocked upgrades |

**Details:**
- Smart contracts are immutable once deployed
- Bugs may result in permanent loss of funds
- Audits reduce but do not eliminate risk
- Novel code has not been battle-tested

### Bridge Risks

**Risk:** Cross-chain bridges may be compromised.

| Bridge | Historical Issues | Our Mitigation |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| Wormhole | $320M exploit (Feb 2022) | Limit per-transfer amounts |
| LayerZero | No major exploits | Gradual rollout |
| Custom bridges | Unknown | Avoid custom solutions |

**Details:**
- Bridge exploits have resulted in billions in losses industry-wide
- We rely on third-party bridge security
- Cross-chain assets may become unrecoverable if bridges fail

### Blockchain Risks

**Risk:** Underlying blockchains may fail or fork.

| Chain | Risk Level | Notes |
|-------|------------|-------|
| Solana | Medium | Network outages in past |
| Ethereum | Low | Battle-tested, but gas volatility |
| L2s (Base, Arbitrum) | Medium | Younger, less proven |
| Lux | High | New network |

| Scenario | Probability | Impact |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Single signer compromise | Medium | No immediate impact (3-of-5) |
| Multiple signer compromise | Low | Treasury drain possible |
| Social engineering | Medium | Depends on target |

### Protocol Exploits

**Risk:** Attackers may find and exploit vulnerabilities.

| Vector | Likelihood | Potential Loss |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| Contract exploit | Low | Up to full treasury |
| Oracle manipulation | Low | Governance manipulation |
| Frontend attack | Medium | User funds |

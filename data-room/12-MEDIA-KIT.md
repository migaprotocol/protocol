## Messaging Guidelines

### Voice and Tone

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Confident | We know what we're building and why |
| Direct | Clear communication, no jargon |
| Inclusive | Welcome to all Persians, globally |
| Serious | This matters—treat it with gravity |
| Hopeful | Optimistic about what we can build together |

### Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Emphasize community ownership (0% team allocation)
- Focus on governance and utility, not speculation
- Acknowledge risks transparently
- Use "we" and "our community"
- Respect the gravity of Iran-related topics

**Don't:**
- Promise returns or financial gains
- Compare to memecoins or get-rich-quick schemes
- Oversimplify complex geopolitical situations
- Use hyperbole about "revolutionizing" things
- Ignore regulatory and sanctions realities

### Terminology

| Preferred | Avoid |
|-----------|-------|
| Pre-sale | ICO, token sale |
| Contribution | Investment |
| Governance token | Asset, coin |
| Community members | Investors, holders |
| DAO treasury | Company funds |

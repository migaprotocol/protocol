# Executive Summary

## MIGA Protocol: A Decentralized Sovereign Wealth Fund for the Persian Diaspora

---

## The Vision

MIGA Protocol is building the first decentralized civil government operating system for the global Persian community. We are creating financial infrastructure that enables 88 million Iranians and 8 million diaspora members to coordinate capital, govern collectively, and fund humanitarian initiatives—transparently, privately, and without intermediaries.

---

## The Problem

### A Stateless Nation Without Financial Infrastructure

The Persian diaspora represents one of the most successful immigrant populations globally:

- **High Net Worth:** Iranian-Americans have median household incomes 50% above the U.S. average
- **Highly Educated:** 57% hold bachelor's degrees or higher (vs. 33% U.S. average)
- **Globally Distributed:** Major communities in US, Canada, UK, Germany, UAE, Australia

Yet this community lacks:

1. **Unified Capital Formation** — No vehicle for collective investment or wealth building
2. **Transparent Governance** — No way to coordinate decisions across borders
3. **Humanitarian Infrastructure** — Fragmented, inefficient aid delivery ($1.3B+ annual remittances through informal channels)
4. **Privacy-Preserving Participation** — No safe way for those inside Iran to participate without exposure

### The Sanctions Paradox

International sanctions intended to pressure the Iranian regime have inadvertently:
- Cut off the diaspora from legitimate financial tools
- Made humanitarian aid delivery nearly impossible
- Pushed remittances into gray markets
- Prevented collective organization

MIGA solves this by creating infrastructure that is:
- Fully transparent (on-chain accountability)
- Sanctions-compliant (NGO structure, no prohibited transactions)
- Privacy-preserving (quantum-safe cryptography protects contributors)

---

## The Solution

### A Three-Layer Architecture

**Layer 1: The MIGA Token**
- Collateral asset representing alignment with the protocol
- Native across 7 blockchains (Solana, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Lux, Bitcoin)
- Zero team allocation—everyone participates equally

**Layer 2: Ten Autonomous DAOs**
- Specialized governance bodies for core functions of civil society
- Each DAO receives automatic funding from protocol fees
- Timelocked, voted, and fully auditable

**Layer 3: Quantum-Safe Privacy**
- Lux FHE (Fully Homomorphic Encryption) for contributor privacy
- Public verification of all fund movements
- Protection for participants in high-threat environments

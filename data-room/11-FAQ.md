## General Questions

### What is MIGA Protocol?

MIGA Protocol is a decentralized civil government operating system for the global Persian community. It consists of:
- **MIGA token:** A governance token for protocol participation
- **Ten DAOs:** Specialized governance bodies for different functions
- **Treasury:** Community-controlled funds for humanitarian and development initiatives
- **Privacy layer:** Quantum-safe privacy for contributor protection

### What does MIGA stand for?

MIGA is a reference to both "Make Iran Great Again" and the Persian word "میگا" (mega/great). It represents the ambition to build infrastructure for Persian collective self-governance.

### Is MIGA a country or government?

No. MIGA is a protocol and community organization, not a nation-state or government. It provides tools for the Persian diaspora to coordinate and govern collective resources, but has no territorial claims or governmental authority.

### Who is behind MIGA?

MIGA is built by a decentralized network of contributors—developers, designers, community organizers, and advisors. There is no traditional company. See the [Team document](./10-TEAM.md) for details.

## Token Questions

### What is the MIGA token used for?

The MIGA token provides:
1. **Governance rights:** Vote on protocol decisions
2. **DAO participation:** Join and contribute to specialized DAOs
3. **Collateral:** Required alongside PARS for maximum voting power
4. **Community membership:** Access to community features

### Is MIGA a security?

No. MIGA is designed as a utility token for governance, not as an investment. There are:
- No dividends or profit sharing
- No promises of returns
- No buyback guarantees
- No team allocation to create insider profits

See [Legal Structure](./09-LEGAL-STRUCTURE.md) for detailed analysis.

### What is the total supply?

7,000,000,000 (7 billion) MIGA tokens, fixed forever.

### How are tokens distributed?

| Allocation | Percentage | Tokens |
|------------|------------|--------|
| DAO Treasury | 50% | 3.5B |
| Pre-Sale | 40% | 2.8B |
| Liquidity | 10% | 700M |

### Why is there no team allocation?

We believe protocols should be community-owned. Team allocations:
- Create insider selling pressure
- Misalign incentives
- Reduce community trust
- Are unnecessary if the protocol succeeds

Core contributors participate through the pre-sale like everyone else.

1. Choose a supported blockchain (7 chains available)
2. Send contribution to the chain's MPC wallet address
3. Your contribution is recorded on-chain
4. At launch (New Year's 2026), claim MIGA proportional to your contribution

### What is the "Race to Nowruz"?

The Race to Nowruz is a competition between the 7 supported chains. Each chain's contributors compete for a share of the 2.8B pre-sale tokens:
- More investment in a chain = more tokens for that chain
- Creates friendly competition between communities
- Ends at Nowruz (March 20, 2025)
- Best prices available during this period

### When does the pre-sale end?

The pre-sale closes at Nowruz (Persian New Year), March 20, 2025.

### When can I claim my tokens?

Tokens will be claimable at launch on New Year's 2026.

The period between Nowruz (March 2025) and launch (January 2026) allows:
- Smart contract development and testing

- Liquidity preparation
- Community building
- Regulatory compliance

## Governance Questions

### How does governance work?

1. **Create proposal:** Stake MIGA to submit proposals
2. **Discussion:** Community debates in forum
3. **Voting:** vePARS holders vote on-chain
4. **Timelock:** Approved proposals wait before execution
5. **Execution:** Proposals automatically execute

### What is vePARS?

vePARS (vote-escrowed PARS) is the governance token created by locking MIGA and PARS together:

```
vePARS = min(PARS, MIGA) × √(lock_duration / max_duration)
```

This formula ensures:
- Need both tokens to have maximum power
- Longer locks = more power
- Large holders can't easily dominate

### What are the 10 DAOs?

| Treasury | Capital management, investments |

Yes. If you don't want to actively vote, you can delegate to trusted community members who will vote on your behalf.

## Privacy Questions

### How does MIGA protect privacy?

1. **Pseudonymous contributions:** Small contributions need no KYC
2. **Shielded voting:** Lux FHE allows encrypted votes
3. **Private transfers:** Optional private transactions
4. **No public contributor lists:** On-chain only

### Why is privacy important?

Contributors inside Iran or other high-risk environments may face:
- Government surveillance
- Retaliation against family members
- Asset seizure
- Legal prosecution

Privacy features protect vulnerable community members.

### Is participation safe for people in Iran?

We cannot guarantee safety. However, MIGA provides:
- Optional anonymity for contributors
- Shielded voting
- No public association required
- Best-practice operational security guidance

**Important:** Consult local legal counsel and assess your personal risk tolerance.

### What about sanctions compliance?

MIGA complies with international sanctions by:
- Screening large contributors against SDN lists
- Not facilitating payments to sanctioned entities
- Operating within humanitarian exemptions
- Maintaining legal counsel specialized in sanctions

See [Legal Structure](./09-LEGAL-STRUCTURE.md) for details.

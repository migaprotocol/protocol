## Treasury Management

### Investment Policy

**Principles:**
1. Capital preservation is primary goal
2. Yield generation secondary
3. Transparency in all positions
4. Diversification across protocols

**Asset Allocation Targets:**

| Asset Class | Target | Range | Rationale |
|-------------|--------|-------|-----------|
| Stablecoins | 50% | 40-60% | Operational stability |
| Blue-chip crypto | 20% | 10-30% | Growth exposure |
| Yield strategies | 20% | 10-30% | Income generation |
| Protocol tokens | 10% | 5-15% | Strategic positions |

**Approved Protocols (Initial):**

| Category | Protocols | Max Allocation |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| Lending | Aave, Compound | 20% each |
| Staking | Lido, Marinade | 20% each |
| LP | Meteora, Uniswap | 15% each |
| Stablecoins | USDC, USDT | 30% each |

### Risk Management

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Smart contract | Max 20% per protocol, audited only |
| Counterparty | Decentralized protocols only |
| Liquidity | Min 30% in liquid stables |
| Concentration | Max 30% per asset |
| Governance | Timelock on all moves |

## Financial Reporting

### Transparency Commitments

1. **Real-time Dashboard:** All treasury positions visible on-chain
2. **Monthly Reports:** Spending and performance summary
3. **Quarterly Reviews:** Community presentation and Q&A
4. **Annual Audit:** Third-party financial review

### Report Contents

**Monthly Report:**
- Opening and closing balances
- Inflows and outflows by category
- Yield earned
- Positions held
- Upcoming expenses

**Quarterly Report:**
- Performance vs. budget
- Grant recipients and outcomes
- Governance decisions
- Strategic updates
- Risk assessment

## Governance Controls

### Spending Approval Matrix

| Amount | Required Approval |
|--------|-------------------|
| <$10,000 | DAO Council (3-of-5) |
| $10,000 - $100,000 | DAO Community Vote |
| $100,000 - $1,000,000 | DAO Vote + Treasury DAO Review |
| >$1,000,000 | Full Protocol Vote (10% quorum) |

### Timelock Requirements

| Action | Minimum Timelock |
|--------|------------------|
| Standard spend | 24 hours |
| Large spend (>$100K) | 48 hours |
| Protocol changes | 7 days |
| Constitutional changes | 14 days |

### Emergency Procedures

Emergency Council (6-of-10 DAO representatives) can:
- Pause treasury withdrawals
- Execute pre-approved emergency actions
- Maximum 72-hour pause without community vote

## Summary

MIGA's treasury management is designed for:

1. **Transparency:** Every dollar tracked on-chain
2. **Accountability:** Multi-layer governance approvals
3. **Sustainability:** Revenue streams exceed operating costs
4. **Impact:** Majority of spending goes to mission (not overhead)
5. **Security:** Conservative investment policy, diversification

100% of contributed funds go to the DAO treasury—not to founders, not to intermediaries, not to marketing agencies. The community governs every allocation decision.
